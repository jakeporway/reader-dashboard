🧠 Scrabble/Crossplay Pro Tips

Two-letter words are GOLD. Memorize the legal 2-letter words. They let you play parallel to existing words and score on multiple lines. Key ones: QI, ZA, XI, XU, JO, KA, AE, OE, OI.
Hook words. Add one letter to an existing word to make a new one. Example: adding S to QUART → QUARTS, or H to EAR → HEAR. Think of it like parasitic scoring.
High-value consonant dumps. Learn short words with J, Q, X, Z: JO, QI (no U needed!), XI, XU, ZA, ZO, ZAP, JAB, JAW, JOT, OX, AX, EX.
-ING, -TION, -ED, -ER, -EST. Always scan your rack for these endings. They turn 3-letter words into 6+ letter words → bonus tiles and bingos.
Rack balance. Keep roughly 3 consonants + 3 vowels + 1 wildcard/flex. If you're vowel-heavy, dump with short vowel words (AA, AE, AI, OE).
The S is precious. Don't waste an S making a basic plural unless it scores 8+ extra points. Save it for hooking onto premium squares.
Bingo bonus! Using all 7 tiles = +50 points. Learn common 7-letter stems: SATIRE+?, RETINA+?, SENIOR+? The letters AEINRST make the most bingos.
Board control. Don't open up triple word scores for your opponent. Play defensively near the edges if Jake is ahead. Block lanes to premium squares.
Prefixes to watch: UN-, RE-, OUT-, OVER-, PRE-, MIS-, DE-. Adding these to existing words on the board can create surprise plays.
The endgame. When the bag is nearly empty, count tiles. If you go out first, you get the value of your opponent's remaining tiles added to your score. Rushing to empty your rack can swing 20+ points.
